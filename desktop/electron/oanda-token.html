Forex & gold prices (OANDA)

Spot XAU/USD, XAG/USD and the forex pairs come from OANDA. A free practice (demo) account is enough.

  1. Open a free practice account at oanda.com.
  2. In the account portal open Manage API Access and generate a token.
  3. Paste it below. It is checked with OANDA, then stored encrypted on this PC.
